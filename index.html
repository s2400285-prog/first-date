MY FIRST DATE LETTER

Dear Julie,

♡

Today was our first date, and honestly, I don't know if words will ever be enough to explain how happy and thankful I am for today.

Thank you for giving me your time. Thank you for spending this day with me, walking with me, eating with me, taking pictures with me, watching Forgotten Island with me, and simply being there beside me.

♡

My favorite part wasn't just the places we went. It was having you beside me.

♡

I also want to thank you for the treats and food you gave for my parents. That honestly touched my heart. You didn't have to do that, but you still thought of them, and I really appreciate that.

I hope you know that I appreciated every little thing you did today. Even the things that might seem small to you meant something to me.

I gave you flowers and a crunchy squishy. I know they aren't expensive or anything fancy, but that's what I could afford right now. Still, I gave them to you with all my heart because I wanted to give you something from me.

🌸

I hope the flowers remind you that you are beautiful, and the little crunchy squishy reminds you of me whenever you play with it.

🌷

There is also something I really want to tell you. I love the way you act. I love your little reactions, your expressions, your sweetness, and the way you can simply be yourself.

And I really, really love the way you hold my hand. ♡

I don't know if you realize how much that simple moment means to me. When you held my hand, it felt so comforting and special. I didn't need anything else at that moment. I was just happy being there with you.

We watched a movie. We ate. We took pictures. We laughed. We walked around. We spent time together. Maybe those things sound simple, but today became something very special to me.

I hope you don't get bored of me as the days pass. I know I can be silly sometimes. I can be clingy. I can be awkward. Sometimes I might not know exactly what to say.

But I promise that I will continue trying to make you smile. I want to keep learning more about you, understanding you, appreciating you, and making more memories with you.

I don't want what I feel for you to only be something temporary. I want to continue loving you day by day.

One day at a time. One memory at a time. One smile at a time.

Thank you for today, Julie. Thank you for your time. Thank you for your sweetness. Thank you for the food. Thank you for the memories. Thank you for holding my hand. And thank you for simply being you.

I appreciate you more than you know. ♡

With all my heart,
James ♡
♡
